Lion · Hills Section · 01 / 05
Nature
& views.

The location of Lion Hills was not chosen at random. We were looking for a place that could combine fast access to central Kyiv with a genuine sense of seclusion in nature.

That is why we chose Lisnyky.

Just 15 minutes to Pechersk, along a direct route with no traffic lights. And at the same time, right beyond the Lion Hills grounds, the Holosiivskyi nature reserve begins, creating an atmosphere of quiet, privacy and harmony with nature.
